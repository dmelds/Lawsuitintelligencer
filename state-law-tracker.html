State legislatures moved faster than Congress on chatbots, baby food and hair relaxers in 2025 and 2026. The statutes they passed split on one question that decides their value to a plaintiff: who holds the claim. Washington and California gave it to the injured user. New York and Georgia gave it to the attorney general alone.

The split matters in pleading. A statute with a private right of action is a count. A statute the state alone enforces is a standard of care that a negligence count can borrow, where the forum allows negligence per se. A shield statute is an affirmative defense waiting in the answer.

The stage and the last move come from each legislature’s record through Open States. The read on each statute is ours. For federal bills, see the Congress Tracker. For the same statutes written for injured readers, see Lawsuit Informer’s state board.
