Congress Tracker

Every federal bill we follow that would change a mass tort, an AI liability fight, litigation funding or qui tam practice. Each row gives the bill's stage, its last committee or floor move, and what it would do to the litigation, rebuilt from Congress.gov three times a day.

The monthly letter carries the month’s moves on this tracker, the Panel’s biggest docket moves and the MDLs you follow, once a month. Sign up →

Most of what Congress does to mass tort litigation happens out of sight. A preemption clause rides in a farm bill. A liability shield is filed as an amendment to the defense bill. A funding disclosure rule surfaces as a stand-alone bill and stalls in committee. This tracker follows each of them from introduction to enactment.

The stage and the last move come from Congress.gov. The last move is the newest action that changes where a bill stands: a committee report, a floor vote, a cloture vote, presentment or signature. Referrals and floor procedure are left out. The one-line read on each bill is ours. For the same bills written for injured readers, see Lawsuit Informer’s Congress board.
