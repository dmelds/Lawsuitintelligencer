[bills]
ca SB 1119|California Adam's Law (SB 1119)|private|2027-01-01|Sets child-safety duties for companion chatbot operators, a crisis protocol among them. A child who suffers actual harm from a violation can sue the operator. Most duties start July 1, 2027.
wa HB 2225|Washington AI companion chatbot law (HB 2225)|private|2027-01-01|Requires disclosure that the chatbot is a machine, safeguards for minors and a self-harm protocol. A violation is an unfair practice under the Consumer Protection Act, which carries a private claim.
nj A 5607|New Jersey chatbot liability bill (A 5607)|private||Would define a chatbot as a product, make the provider strictly liable for injuries it causes, give injured people a claim and limit use of chat logs. Drawn from the EPIC, CFA and Fairplay model bill.
ny S 9051|New York AI companion law for minors (S 9051-B)|state|2027-01-01|Bars operators from offering minors an AI companion with any of 12 listed features. The attorney general enforces it at up to $25,000 per violation. The signed text dropped the private right of action in the January draft.
ga SB 540|Georgia conversational AI safety law (SB 540)|state|2027-07-01|Requires AI disclosure, a block on sexually explicit exchanges and simulated romance with minors, and a self-harm protocol. Enforcement belongs to the attorney general alone.
id S 1297|Idaho Conversational AI Safety Act (S 1297)|state|2027-07-01|Requires AI disclosure and protections for minors. Published summaries say the attorney general enforces it and the act creates no private right of action.
ny A 1797|New York hair relaxer warning label law (A 1797)|state||Requires a warning label on any hair relaxer sold in New York that contains a carcinogen or a reproductive toxicant. The attorney general enforces it. The label duty starts 18 months after the November 21, 2025 signing.
ca SB 236|California C.U.R.L. Act (SB 236)|state||Bars making or selling hair relaxers that contain listed chemicals, formaldehyde among them. The Department of Toxic Substances Control sets test methods, registers makers and enforces the ban.
ga SB 144|Georgia pesticide failure-to-warn shield (SB 144)|shield||Bars failure-to-warn liability in Georgia for a pesticide whose label carries the warnings EPA requires. Georgia followed North Dakota. The AP reported that it does not reach cases already filed.
ca SB 867|California AI toy moratorium (SB 867)|unread||Bars making or selling a toy with a built-in companion chatbot in California until January 1, 2031.
ri HB 7350|Rhode Island AI companion models law (HB 7350)|unread|2027-01-01|Requires AI companion tools to disclose what they are and to keep a protocol for users who talk about suicide or harming others. The Senate twin is S 2195.
va HB 1844|Virginia Baby Food Protection Act (HB 1844)|unread|2026-01-01|Bars the sale of baby food that exceeds FDA limits for toxic heavy metals. Makers must test each product monthly, post the results and put a scannable code on the label.
ca AB 56|California social media warning label law (AB 56)|unread|2027-01-01|Requires platforms to show users under 18 a mental health warning at first use each day, again after three hours and every hour after that.
pa HB 507|Pennsylvania Baby Food Protection Act (HB 507)|unread||Would have the state test each batch of baby food made in Pennsylvania and put results on the label. Caps: arsenic 10 ppb, lead and cadmium 5 ppb, mercury 2 ppb.
pa HB 2883|Pennsylvania PFAS pesticide ban (HB 2883)|unread||Would bar the sale and use of pesticides that contain PFAS when safer alternatives are available.
